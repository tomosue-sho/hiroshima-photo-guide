What is Hiroshima Kagura?

Kagura is a traditional Japanese performing art with deep connections to Shinto rituals and mythology.

Hiroshima Kagura developed its own distinctive style in the Hiroshima region. Performances combine traditional stories with dramatic costumes, elaborate masks, powerful music, and highly expressive movement.

Hiroshima Kagura is also known for its accessibility and entertainment value. While some performances and venues retain a solemn and traditional atmosphere, others are lively events enjoyed by local communities and visitors alike.

How to Experience Kagura

🎭 Masks & Costumes

Masks and costumes help reveal who a character really is. A performer may even transform dramatically during a scene.

🥁 Music & Movement

Drums, flutes, chanting, and highly energetic movements create the rhythm and atmosphere of the performance.

📖 Stories

Many performances are based on Japanese mythology, legends, historical figures, and famous stories.
